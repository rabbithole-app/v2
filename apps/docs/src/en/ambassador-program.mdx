# Ambassador Program

The Ambassador Program turns a recommendation into recurring income: everyone
who joins Rabbithole through your link earns you **15% of every payment they
make** — for as long as they stay. The program is open to every Rabbithole
user; there are no applications or audience requirements.

:::tip{title="Short version"}

You share a link from the **Ambassador** page in your account menu. People who
join through it become your referrals: you receive 15% of their payments, and
a coupon link additionally gives them 10% off their first payments. Earnings
accrue to your Rabbithole wallet balance and can be withdrawn at any time.

:::

## Start in three steps

Everything you need lives on one dashboard page.

1. Open the **Ambassador** page from your account menu in the dashboard.
2. Create a coupon and copy its link.
3. Share the link. Everyone who joins through it becomes your referral, and
   their payments start counting toward your earnings.

## Coupon links and your personal link

There are two kinds of links, and they differ in what the invitee gets.

A **coupon link** carries a discount: whoever uses it gets **10% off** their
first [Starter Vault](/how-it-works/payment#starter-vault-and-rabbithole-pro)
and their first month of [Rabbithole Pro](/how-it-works/pro). The discount
gives your recommendation a concrete benefit for the invitee, and applying the
coupon is what links them to you. You control how a coupon works: single-use
for a personal invite, unlimited for an audience, with or without an expiry
date, and with a private note to tell your coupons apart.

Your **personal referral link** is permanent: it never expires and never runs
out. It links people to you the same way, but without a discount. Use it where
you can't rotate coupons — a blog post, a profile, a signature.

## What you earn

You receive **15% of every payment** your referrals make: Starter Vault
purchases, Rabbithole Pro subscriptions, and every renewal. Wallet balance
top-ups are not payments and don't produce a share.

Earnings arrive on your Rabbithole wallet balance in the same tokens your
referrals paid with, and you can withdraw them whenever you like. The
Ambassador page shows your referrals, coupon usage, and earnings in one place.

## Good to know

- Your share is calculated from the amount the referral actually paid, so
  their discount doesn't reduce your share disproportionately.
- A person is linked to one ambassador, once. The first link or coupon they
  use decides the attribution, and it doesn't change afterwards.
- If someone joined through your permanent link and missed the discount, they
  can still apply one of your coupons before their first payment.
