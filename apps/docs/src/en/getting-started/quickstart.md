## Get Started

1. Visit [rabbithole.app](https://rabbithole.app)
2. Click **Login** and authenticate with [Internet Identity](https://id.ai/)
3. **Create your storage** — pay a fixed fee, and your personal canister is deployed on the blockchain in about a minute
4. Start working with your files:

| What you can do | |
|---|---|
| Upload and download files | Organize with folders |
| Rename, move, and delete | Share files and folders with other users |

Rabbithole is built around end-to-end encryption. When encryption is enabled for your storage flow, files are protected before upload.

:::tip{title="Want to know more?"}

Read [How Encryption Works](/en/how-it-works/encryption) to understand how your files are protected, or [Data Sovereignty](/en/how-it-works/sovereignty) to learn why you truly own your data.

:::
