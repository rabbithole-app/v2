## Release Notes

This release makes wallet funding and account navigation clearer in the storage app.

### Wallet

- You can now deposit supported Internet Computer tokens to your Principal ID.
- The wallet now shows which Internet Computer funds are on your Principal ID and which funds are already available in your Rabbithole balance.
- You can now withdraw supported wallet balances to Internet Computer, Base, and Solana wallets.

### Navigation

- Account settings, subscription, and wallet screens now support direct links.
- Refreshing or opening storage app links directly works more reliably.
